Frequently Asked Questions

Is PixelFlow really 100% private and free?

Yes! Check your browser's Network tab: no image file is ever uploaded to any server. All processing runs right on your computer using native HTML5 Canvas, FileReader, and WebAssembly APIs.

Can I reorder pages when making a PDF?

Absolutely! When you upload multiple images to the Image to PDF tool, you can freely drag cards around or use the friendly left/right arrow buttons to customize the final PDF page order.

Can I download multiple converted files as a ZIP?

Yes! Whenever you convert 2 or more files, PixelFlow activates the "Download All as .ZIP" button to package everything into a single download right inside your browser.
